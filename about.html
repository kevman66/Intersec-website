Introduction

Who we are

Intersec Security Group Ltd is a nationally operating security services provider headquartered in Ntinda, Kampala, with regional branches in Jinja, Mbarara, and other key locations across Uganda. With many years of operational experience, the company has built a strong reputation for delivering reliable, technology-driven security solutions tailored to diverse client needs.

Our services include professional security guarding, electronic surveillance systems, alarm monitoring, investigations, event security, risk assessments, and escort services. We serve government institutions, corporate organizations, NGOs, industrial facilities, commercial entities, and residential clients nationwide.

Intersec distinguishes itself through an integrated security approach that combines highly trained personnel, modern technology, intelligence-led operations, rapid response capability, and a strong commitment to guard welfare and professionalism. We remain dedicated to safeguarding life and property with integrity, efficiency, and excellence.

Our Security Personnel

Quality begins before deployment

The quality of our service begins long before deployment. At Intersec Security Group Ltd, rigorous recruitment and structured training form the foundation of our operations.

We receive over 250 applications each month but select an average of only 12 candidates, reflecting our strict selection standards. All successful recruits undergo comprehensive training at our facility in Jinja, in line with Government of Uganda and Uganda Police requirements — ensuring our personnel are disciplined, professional, and fully prepared to protect life and property.

Applicant Screening Criteria

What it takes to wear the uniform

Neat, presentable, with height not less than 1.7m and an extended chest.

No physical disabilities and mentally sound.

Aged between 20–45 years, with two eligible guarantors.

Fluent spoken & written English, with no criminal record.

Amenable to discipline — observant, intelligent, good comportment.

Investigations officers must have a detective background in government or equivalent service.
